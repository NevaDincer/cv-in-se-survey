# On Improving Management of Duplicate Video-Based Bug Reports

- **Year:** 2024
- **Venue/Source:** IEEE/ACM 46th International Conference on Software Engineering: Companion Proceedings (ICSE-Companion '24), Doctoral Symposium track
- **DOI:** 10.1145/3639478.3639786

## Metadata

- **se_area:** Maintenance (bug report management)
- **se_task:** Duplicate detection for video-based bug reports (mobile GUI bug videos)
- **cv_technique:** Proposed (not yet implemented): self-supervised Vision Transformers (e.g., DINO-style ViT) for visual scene-layout feature extraction, aggregated into a Bag-of-Visual-Words representation; scene-text detection/recognition models (EAST, CRAFT, TrOCR) for extracting on-screen text from frames; multimodal Transformers to jointly fuse visual and textual modalities
- **platform:** Mobile (Android applications)
- **rationale:** Context-driven (capturing nuanced GUI visual/textual patterns that indicate the same underlying bug) and robustness (adapting scene-learning capabilities of ViTs to subtle visual variations across recordings of the same bug)
- **artifact_type:** Temporal (subsampled video frames) and full-interface (GUI screens depicted in those frames)
- **eval_method:** Proposed evaluation only: standard information-retrieval metrics (mean reciprocal rank, mean average precision) on an extended version of a prior benchmark (Cooper et al.), enriched with real (non-synthetic) bugs from the AndroR2+ dataset; no results are reported in this abstract
- **challenges:** Scarcity of labeled data for training/evaluating duplicate video-bug-report detection; prior benchmarks rely mostly on synthetic/injected faults rather than real-world bugs; rich pixel-based video data is costly and complex to analyze automatically compared to text-based reports

## Summary

This is a doctoral-symposium extended abstract outlining a PhD research plan (not yet executed) for improving duplicate detection of video-based mobile bug reports. The author motivates the problem by noting the rapid growth of video-based bug reports in mobile issue trackers (e.g., GitHub Issues, native Android screen recording) and the difficulty of automatically comparing the "nuanced," pixel-rich visual patterns such videos contain. Building directly on prior work by Cooper et al. (2021), which combined visual (CNN + Bag-of-Visual-Words) and textual (OCR + Bag-of-Words) similarity for duplicate video bug report retrieval, the proposed approach aims to replace the CNN/ResNet-based visual backbone with self-supervised Vision Transformers, whose hierarchical attention-based features are hypothesized to better capture GUI scene layout, and to replace the heuristic OCR pipeline with more recent scene-text detectors/recognizers (EAST, CRAFT, TrOCR). The plan further proposes fusing visual and textual signals via multimodal Transformers rather than simple similarity aggregation, and constructing a more realistic benchmark by recording additional videos for real (not synthetic) bugs drawn from the AndroR2+ dataset of reproduced Android bug reports. Because this is a research proposal rather than a completed study, it contains no experiments, implementation details, or quantitative results, only a described research design and anticipated contributions.
