- **cv_technique:** Grad-CAM (activation heatmap generation for CNN explainability)
- **platform:** General

- **artifact_type:** Natural image

## Summary

The CV model itself is what is being tested here. Grad-CAM heatmaps reveal which pixels the model activates on, detecting biased training. Proposed as an MLOps pipeline segment. A workshop paper, at proof-of-concept level.
