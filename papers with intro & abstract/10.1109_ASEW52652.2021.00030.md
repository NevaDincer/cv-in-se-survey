# Classification of UML Diagrams to Support Software Engineering Education

- **Year:** 2021
- **Venue/Source:** ASEW 2021
- **DOI:** 10.1109/ASEW52652.2021.00030

## Metadata

- **se_area:** Requirements
- **se_task:** UML diagram type classification for accessibility in SE education
- **cv_technique:** CNN (VGG16; ResNet50; InceptionV3) with transfer learning and data augmentation
- **platform:** General
- **rationale:** Context-driven
- **artifact_type:** Document
- **eval_method:** Classification accuracy on 6 UML diagram types; transfer learning vs baseline comparison
- **challenges:** Scarce labeled UML diagram data; class diagram most difficult to classify

## Summary

Accessibility-motivated: classifies UML diagrams as first step toward making didactic materials accessible for visually impaired students. Transfer learning with VGG16/ResNet50/InceptionV3 on a new 6-class UML dataset.

Adjacent to [[10.1109_MODELS-C59198.2023.00099]] and [[10.1016_j.jss.2022.111431]] but educational accessibility angle is unique to it.
