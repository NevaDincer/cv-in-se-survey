## Summary

Uses A3C reinforcement learning directly on GUI screenshots, needing no DOM or accessibility tree, just pixels in and actions out. Achieves up to 6x better exploration efficiency than baseline algorithms and rivals experienced human testers. A strong case for purely vision-driven test agents, and a useful contrast against tools that rely on widget metadata.
